Files you open from elsewhere

Kinestry can open a family file another app hands it — a Kinestry backup, or a Family Tree Builder .ftz archive. It always opens as a new family tree, so the tree you already have is not overwritten or merged into.

Two things are worth knowing. A file is only as good as whoever made it: if it is damaged, Kinestry refuses it before touching anything, and if parts of it do not make sense it tells you what it had to leave out. And a file from someone else may contain personal information about living people. Whether you have the right to hold that information is your decision, not ours.
