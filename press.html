Everything you need to write about Kinestry

In one sentence: Kinestry is a family tree app for Android that keeps the whole tree on your phone, with no account, no cloud, no ads and no tracking.

The facts

  • Made by Valeed Labs, one independent developer.
  • Platform: Android, from Google Play. Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • Records births, marriages and deaths with the precision you know (a year, a month or a full date), where someone was born, lived and died, photos and notes, and marriages that ended in divorce or separation.
  • Prints the tree as a PDF on A4, Letter, A3, A1 or A0, or on one full-size page, and exports PNG and GEDCOM.
  • Shares a copy of the tree, photos included, as a file through any app; relatives open it in Kinestry as their own tree.
  • Desktop Mode opens the tree in a laptop browser over the same Wi-Fi, with the phone serving it.
  • Names in many scripts, including Arabic, Hebrew, Indian scripts, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Cyrillic and Greek. The app’s menus are in English.

What it deliberately does not do

No accounts, no servers, no cloud sync, no record search and no DNA. The tree exists only on the phone it was built on, so its owner keeps their own backups. That trade is the point of the app, and worth saying plainly in anything written about it. The privacy policy sets out exactly what touches the network.
